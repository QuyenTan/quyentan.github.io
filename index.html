About Us

C-MoRe (Clinical Motor Recovery assessment tool) was developed by UC Davis computer science researchers Ziqiang "Joe" Zhu and Jun Min Kim, alongside project lead Andria Farrens and UC Irvine's Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. The project began when Farrens, while researching robotic hand rehabilitation for stroke survivors, saw an opportunity to combine smartphone camera footage with motion-tracking software to quantify recovery more precisely than a clinician's eye alone. Today, C-MoRe uses computer vision to analyze patients performing the Box and Blocks Test, giving physicians objective, detailed data — like grasp duration and movement velocity — to personalize rehabilitation and move toward more precise, data-driven stroke care.
